Economic Impacts of Transportation Outages
The economic implications of transportation outages are considerable. A report released by the Urban Transportation Commission on December 7, 2023, highlighted that unexpected service disruptions in major cities could lead to losses amounting to millions of dollars in a single day due to decreased productivity, lost commerce, and the ripple effects on associated industries.
