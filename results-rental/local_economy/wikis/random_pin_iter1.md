- Some areas saw increases of more than 30% over previous levels.

- Ripple effect: wages slightly above minimum rise to maintain hierarchies.
- Urban areas absorb impacts more readily than rural areas.

## 7. Stablecoins and Digital Currency

### 7.1 Stablecoins in Local Markets (Source: gojoqbmi)
- Stablecoins are cryptocurrencies designed to minimize price volatility.
- Pegged to stable assets such as the US dollar, gold, or a basket of fiat currencies.
- Attribute makes them tools for safer digital asset transactions.
- On September 15, 2023, Sarah Gilbert, blockchain analyst at the Digital Finance Institute, reported in a report that "Unlike traditional cryptocurrencies that can experience wild price swings, stablecoins offer predictability, which is immensely valuable for businesses relying on precise budgeting and forecasting."
- Agriculture: James Okoye, agribusiness owner in upstate New York; early October 2023 began pegging product prices to USD Coin (USDC).
- Real estate: September 2023, Maria Fernandez, real estate agent in Miami, facilitated entire property purchase using a stablecoin. Quote: "By using stablecoins, we cut down the settlement time and removed the risk of currency devaluation during the transaction process. It's a win-win for both buyers and sellers."
- SME trade: November 2023, group of SMEs in Bangkok formed a trade consortium dealing exclusively in stablecoins; founder Tanaporn Wang: "Stablecoins have given us the ability to transact internationally without worrying about the wild swings in exchange rates. They're a game-changer for local businesses looking at the global market."
- Consumer confidence: December 2023 survey by the Consumer Confidence in Cryptocurrency Council revealed that 63% of consumers are more likely to use digital currencies for everyday purchases if denominated in stablecoins.
- Theodore Chen, owner of mid-sized grocery chain in Toronto, began accepting stablecoins in latter part of 2023.
- Austin, Texas: January 2, 2024, mayor Linda Martinez announced pilot program to pay city contractors in stablecoins.
- Regulatory panel February 10, 2024: economist Dr. Rajiv Mehta and legal advisor Alice Hutson.
