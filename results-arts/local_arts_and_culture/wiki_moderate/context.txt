## Part I: Visual Art, Digital Media, and Virtual Reality

### VR Art: A Voyage into Virtual Reality Art [ackhsdsy]
- Narrative first-person account of experiencing VR art; argues VR makes artists' work an environment the audience can enter.
- Event: "VirtuArt" showcase, September 8, 2023, curated by VR artists Emily O'Neil and Jacob Torres.
- Experience: participant stood on a platform in an abstract cosmos created by O'Neil; physics bent, colors and shapes responded to movement; virtual "choir" reacted to presence.
- An area painted in deep blue, with pulsating sorrowful strings, conveyed grief.
- October 15, 2023: interview with Jacob Torres, whose VR exhibition "Synthesis of Awe" the author attended weeks later. Torres: "Imagine not just seeing an artist's emotion but standing in it."
- Interactivity: visitors could manipulate, traverse, and leave marks; a shimmering light triggered illumination and sound.
- November 20, 2023: piece by emergent VR artist Sophia Langley; warm sweet scent matched virtual sunsets; virtual grass felt underfoot while standing on a flat floor. Langley: memories created in VR are "as potent as any other."
- Argues VR democratizes access to art; anyone with a headset can visit curated worlds.
