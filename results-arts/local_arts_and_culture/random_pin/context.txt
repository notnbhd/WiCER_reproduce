## 1. Virtual Reality, Film & Digital Media

### 1.1 VR Art Showcases (ackhsdsy)
- **Event:** "VirtuArt" showcase, September 8, 2023, curated by VR artists Emily O'Neil and Jacob Torres.
- **Setting:** Platform suspended in an abstract cosmos created by O'Neil; physics "bent," colors and shapes responded to visitor movement.
- **Emotional segment:** Area painted in deep blue, with echoing sorrowful strings, described as an "ocean of grief."
- **Torres interview, October 15, 2023:** Discussed his VR exhibition "Synthesis of Awe," attended a few weeks later. Quote: visitors can "stand in" an artist's emotion.
- **Sophia Langley:** Emergent VR artist; piece on November 20, 2023 included a warm, sweet scent matching visual sunsets and virtual grass underfoot.

### 1.2 VR Filmmaking (dznaltfk)
- **Core concept:** Cinematic experiences using 360-degree environments; viewers look in any direction.
- **Oculix 3000:** 360-degree camera introduced by cinematographer Emily Jacobsen in December 2023; real-time stitching software.
- **"Whispers of the Ancients":** Award-winning VR experience by screenwriter Roberto García, released February 10, 2024.
- **Directing:** "Directing in the round"; no hiding place for crew; VR pre-visualization software used.
- **Acting:** Actress Anna Liu, interview October 5, 2023, on VR "Midsummer Night's Dream": "theater in the round on steroids."
- **Editing:** Editor Michael Zhang, "Orbit's Odyssey," launched September 17, 2023, guided viewers by spatial audio and visual cues.
- **Documentary:** "Through Their Eyes," released November 1, 2023, placed viewers in lives of refugees.

- **Challenges:** Production cost, motion sickness, specialized headsets.

### 5.2 Culinary Heritage: Top Ten Dishes (bymllyqb)
1. **Paella Valenciana** (Valencia, Spain): mid-1800s; saffron rice; communal open-flame cooking.
2. **Tagine** (Morocco): stew since late 9th century; Berber, Arab, Andalusian influences.
3. **Nasi Lemak** (Malaysia): declared national dish October 10, 2023 by Malaysia Culinary Heritage Association; banana leaf; coconut rice, sambal, anchovies, peanuts, egg.
4. **Gumbo** (Louisiana): early 18th century; West African, Native American, French, Spanish influences; okra, filé powder, andouille.
5. **Feijoada** (Brazil): Journalist Carlos Mendes article "Feijoada: A Taste of Freedom," September 7, 2024; dates to 1500s; black beans with pork and beef.
6. **Sushi** (Japan): origins as fish preservation, 4th century BC; Narezushi; Edo period (1603–1868) nigiri.
7. **Rogan Josh** (Kashmir): Persian influence around 15th century; lamb curry; Kashmiri chillies.
8. **Moussaka** (Greece): roots in Ottoman Empire era; eggplant, minced meat, béchamel.
9. **Peking Duck** (China): first documented in Yuan Dynasty; historian Wang Zhi's 2023 dissertation.
10. **Borscht** (Ukraine): traces to 14th-century traditions; beetroot, cabbage, potatoes.
