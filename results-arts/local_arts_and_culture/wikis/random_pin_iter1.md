# Arts & Culture Community Reference Wiki

## 1. Performance Art

### 1.1 The New Wave of Performance Art: Challenging Conventions [hmgsvxnd]
- **Alec Ramirez**, 24, transformed an abandoned warehouse into an immersive "theater" on **October 15** (2023) that questioned the boundaries between performer and audience. His piece "Converse in Verse" engaged passersby as both spectators and contributors.
- **Maria Zhang**'s "Rooftop Rhythms," initiated **November 29**, staged performances on city building rooftops (interpretive dance, spoken word) against the skyline.
- **James "Lightfoot" Harris** and crew fuse breakdance, krumping, and martial arts. Performance "Battlefield Within" premiered **December 3**.
- **Chloe Park**'s "Pavement Patterns" (debut **September 21**): unannounced, flash-mob-style sidewalk performances with a dancer troupe on connectivity and urban isolation.
- **DJ Vex**: impromptu turntable gigs, notably **October 5** at City Park, remixing everyday sounds.
- **Emma Liu**: "Silent Crescendo," premiered **November 12**, contrasting silence with bursts of sound.
- **Leo Torres**: "Dialogues in Motion," introduced **September 10**; viewers move alongside performers.
- **Katie Simmons**: "Sensory Threads," launched **December 16**; audience affects outcome through touch, sound, scent.
- **Julian Fletcher**: "Virtually Yours," unrolled **September 30**, a VR experience whose story altered with participants' decisions.
- **Zara Patel**: "Insta-Moments," running since **September 17**, uses Instagram to document street performances and audience reactions.
- **Benjamin Brooks**: "Urban Echoes," **November 22**, on homelessness.
- **Luna Martinez**: "Chroma Chronicles," **December 13**, multicultural identity through costumes, music, movement.

### 1.2 The Shock Revolution: Unveiling the Radical New Wave of Performance Art [yxybhwyo]
- **Dakota Jones** launched interactive series "Synapses Alight" on **September 3, 2023** at a small gallery.
- **Alejandra Herrera**: "Echoes of the Unseen," opening **October 21**; sensory labyrinth on anxieties.
- Venues: abandoned factory, desolate underpass, public library corner.
- **Marion Dupont**: "freeze mobs," **September 15**; 50 individuals froze for five minutes in a city square.
- **Vincent Zhao**: "Rebirth through Fire," **November 11**, in a neighborhood park; artist emerged from flames.
- **Luna García**: "Quantum Lives," **December 8**; participants assigned roles, leaping through historical moments.
- "Breathing Spaces" (**January 12**): public transit hubs became storytelling stages.
- **Eliza Kwon**: "Flesh and Binary," **February 20**; dance, light, live coding on identity and technology.
- **Jayden Clarke**: "Vestiges of the Disavowed."

### 1.3 The New Wave of Performance Art: Challenging Conventions (digital) [jslrqdze]

### 6.2 Culinary Heritage: The Story of Communities (top 10 dishes) [bymllyqb]
1. **Paella Valenciana**: Valencia; first crafted mid-1800s; saffron rice.
2. **Tagine**: Morocco; staple since late 9th century; Berbers, Arabs, Andalusian settlers.
3. **Nasi Lemak**: On **October 10, 2023**, Malaysia Culinary Heritage Association declared it national dish; banana leaf; coconut rice, sambal, anchovies, peanuts, egg.
4. **Gumbo**: Louisiana; early 18th century; okra, filé powder, andouille.
5. **Feijoada**: Brazil; journalist **Carlos Mendes**, "Feijoada: A Taste of Freedom," **September 7, 2024**; dates to 1500s.
6. **Sushi**: origins 4th century BC; Edo period (1603-1868) narezushi to nigiri.
7. **Rogan Josh**: Kashmir; Persian influence ~15th century; Kashmiri chillies.
8. **Moussaka**: Greece; Ottoman roots; eggplant, minced meat, béchamel.
9. **Peking Duck**: China; Yuan Dynasty; historian **Wang Zhi**, 2023 dissertation.
10. **Borscht**: Ukraine; 14th-century traditions; beetroot, cabbage, potatoes.
