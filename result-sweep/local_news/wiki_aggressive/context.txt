## 1. Small Business and Entrepreneurship

### City Small Business Support Program [ajowsdgn]
- Announced by City Council on September 15, 2023.
- Developed after town hall meetings earlier in 2023. Spearheaded by Councilwoman Marjorie Reynolds; championed by Mayor Lucas O'Reilly.
- Components: (1) Seed funding, competitive grants for start-up costs; (2) Mentorship and free or subsidized training in business planning, marketing, financial management; (3) Networking events linking entrepreneurs with investors, suppliers, clients; (4) Technology support: website development, e-commerce, social media.
- Economic Development Director Javier Suarez helped design the program. Small Business Liaison Officer Sandra Chen guides applicants.
- Eligibility: businesses under two years old, a concrete business plan, city residence. Priority for women, minorities, veterans.
- Applications were open via the official city website, which hosts the form and a documentation guide.
- Feedback quoted: aspiring bakery owner Jasmine Li; tech innovator Raj Patel.

## 2. Transit and Mobility

### City Council Gives Green Light to Transform Public Transit [dbmbfblh]
- Approved September 15, 2023. Spearheaded by Councilwoman Angela Martinez and Councilman David Cho.
- 500-page proposal. Total headline investment: $1.2 billion.
- Light rail expansion focused on connecting underserved areas to the central business district.
- Fleet of hybrid and electric buses.
- Infrastructure modernization: upgrading old rail tracks, station renovations, real-time updates, mobile ticketing and smart card integration.
- ADA compliance across facilities: tactile paving, audio signals, wider gates.
- Proposed fare subsidies for low-income residents, students, and the elderly.

### City Council Unanimously Greenlights Grand Transit Overhaul [qinywfpd]
- Approved September 15, 2023. Councilwoman Patricia Gomez and Transportation Committee Chair Johnathan Hughes led.
- Capital outlay $1.2 billion over the transformation lifespan (City Treasurer Michael O'Donnell). Funded by state grants, municipal bonds, and a slight local sales tax increase.
- Phased over five years.
- Councilman Derek Li: "an economic renaissance through transportation reform."
- Plans: environmentally friendly buses, rail expansion, service frequency increase, advanced ticketing.
- Incentives: discounted monthly passes; key performance indicators for mid-course corrections.

### City Council Votes for Comprehensive Recycling Mandates [zecqwbip]
- Session dated September 14, 2024 (as stated in document). Council Chair Joan Montgomery.
- History: early 2000s volunteer-based recycling; collection bins for paper, glass, metal; curbside pilots.
- Environmental Committee chair Thomas Krieger led push.
