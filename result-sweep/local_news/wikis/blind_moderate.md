## 1. Small Business and Entrepreneurship

### 1.1 City Small Business Support Program
- Announced by City Council on **Sept 15, 2023**. Developed after town hall meetings held earlier in 2023. [ajowsdgn]
- Led by **Councilwoman Marjorie Reynolds**; championed by **Mayor Lucas O'Reilly**.
- **Economic Development Director Javier Suarez** designed the program. **Small Business Liaison Officer Sandra Chen** guides applicants.
- Components: (1) Seed funding, competitive grants for start-up costs; (2) Mentorship and free or subsidized training in business planning, marketing, financial management; (3) Networking events connecting entrepreneurs with investors, suppliers, clients; (4) Technology support: website development, e-commerce, social media.
- Eligibility: businesses under two years old, concrete business plan, located within city limits. Priority for women, minorities, veterans.
- Applications via official city website, with form and document guide.
- Beneficiary quotes: Jasmine Li (aspiring bakery owner); Raj Patel (tech innovator).

- [aypeaatz] Urban Transit Authority (UTA) study released **Sept 15, 2023**: nearly **30%** of urban dwellers consider FMLM issues a significant deterrent.

- **Michael Gomez**: CEO of local Public Transit Authority (PTA), took office 2023. Launched after **Oct 1, 2023**: pilot syncing bus schedules with train arrivals and departures.

### 4.1 City Council Approves Game-Changing Zoning Laws
- [cjjjljda] Laws effective since **Sept 15, 2023**; meetings culminated **Oct 12, 2023**.
- **Councilwoman Jessica Morales** and urban planning expert **Dr. Henry L. Gates** led.
- Measures: increased density in single-family zones; mandatory inclusionary zoning (a percentage of units affordable).
