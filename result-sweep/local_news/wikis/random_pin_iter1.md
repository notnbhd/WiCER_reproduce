## 1. Small Business Support and Entrepreneurship

### 1.1 City Small Business Support Program [ajowsdgn]
- Announced by City Council on September 15, 2023. Aims to foster economic growth and diversify the local economy.
- Developed after town hall meetings held earlier in 2023. Spearheaded by Councilwoman Marjorie Reynolds; championed by Mayor Lucas O'Reilly.
- Economic Development Director Javier Suarez shaped program design. Small Business Liaison Officer Sandra Chen guides applicants and connects them to the City Council.
- Components:
  - Seed funding: competitive grants for start-up costs.
  - Mentorship and training: partnerships with seasoned entrepreneurs; free or subsidized sessions on business planning, marketing, financial management.
  - Networking events connecting entrepreneurs with investors, suppliers, clients.
  - Technology support: website development, e-commerce, social media.
- Eligibility: businesses less than two years old, with a concrete business plan, within city limits. Focus on underrepresented groups: women, minorities, veterans.
- Application form and guide hosted on the official city website; process described as streamlined.
- Feedback quoted: aspiring bakery owner Jasmine Li; tech innovator Raj Patel ("The mentorship aspect of the program brings a sense of community").
