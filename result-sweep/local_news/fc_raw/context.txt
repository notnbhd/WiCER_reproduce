Richards goes on to delineate some of the innovative approaches currently being piloted in various urban locales. These range from subsidized e-bike and e-scooter programs to partnerships with rideshare services for providing last-mile solutions. Richards emphasizes the importance of flexibility and adaptability in these initiatives to cater to the diverse needs of urban populations.
