## 1. Economy and Small Business Programs

### Small Business Support Program (City Council, announced September 15, 2023)
- Announced by City Council on Sept 15, 2023 to support new and emerging businesses.
- Grew out of a series of town hall meetings held earlier in 2023.
- Spearheaded by Councilwoman Marjorie Reynolds; championed by Mayor Lucas O'Reilly.
- Components:
  - Seed funding: competitive grants for start-up costs.
  - Mentorship and training: partnerships with seasoned entrepreneurs; free or subsidized sessions on business planning, marketing, financial management.
  - Networking events connecting entrepreneurs with investors, suppliers and clients.
  - Technology support: website development, e-commerce, social media.
- Economic Development Director Javier Suarez helped design the program.
- Small Business Liaison Officer Sandra Chen guides applicants and links them to the City Council.
- Eligibility: businesses less than two years old, with a concrete business plan, within city limits. Focus on women, minorities and veterans.
- Application form and guide hosted on the official city website; applications open at time of announcement.
- Feedback: aspiring bakery owner Jasmine Li said resources helped her get her business plan off the ground; tech innovator Raj Patel praised the mentorship.

## 4. Transportation and Mobility

### Public Transit System Upgrade, Council Approval (Doc dbmbfblh)
- City Council voted on Sept 15, 2023 for a comprehensive transit upgrade.
- Detailed in a 500-page proposal; spearheaded by Councilwoman Angela Martinez and Councilman David Cho.
- Total headline budget $1.2 billion.
- Major share: expanding light rail, connecting underserved areas to the central business district.
- Fleet of hybrid and electric buses.
- Infrastructure: upgrading old rail tracks, renovating stations, real-time updates, mobile ticketing and smart card integration.
- ADA compliance: tactile paving, audio signals, wider gates.
- Proposed fare subsidies for low-income residents, students and elderly.
- Goals: reduce traffic congestion, cut emissions, create thousands of jobs (construction and operation).

### Grand Transit Overhaul (Doc qinywfpd)
- Approved by City Council Sept 15, 2023; led by Councilwoman Patricia Gomez and Transportation Committee Chair Johnathan Hughes.
- Capital outlay $1.2 billion over lifespan, from state grants, municipal bonds and a slight sales tax increase; City Treasurer Michael O'Donnell.
- Phased over five years.
- Councilman Derek Li: "economic renaissance through transportation reform."
- Benefits: lower fuel costs, reduced road maintenance, productivity from shorter commutes, increased routes and frequency to diminish "travel deserts."
- Incentives: discounted monthly passes; key performance indicators for mid-course corrections.

- Gomez: PTA initiatives launched after Oct 1, 2023.
