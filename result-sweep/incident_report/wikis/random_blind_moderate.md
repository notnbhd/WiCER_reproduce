- **Fire Chief Justin Hartley** and **Sergeant Emily Rosario** (police) coordinated in first 48 hours.
- **AAR conducted Oct 15, 2023;** over two days; led by responders, civic officials, external analysts.

- **Regional emergency management symposium:** January 20, 2024.
