### 3.2 Culinary Heritage: Top 10 Dishes (bymllyqb)
1. Paella Valenciana: Valencia, first crafted mid-1800s; saffron rice.
2. Tagine, Morocco: stew since late 9th century; Berbers, Arabs, Andalusians.
3. Nasi Lemak, Malaysia: Malaysia Culinary Heritage Association declared national dish Oct 10, 2023; banana leaf.
4. Gumbo, Louisiana: since early 18th century; okra, filé, andouille.
5. Feijoada, Brazil: Carlos Mendes article "Feijoada: A Taste of Freedom," Sept 7, 2024; dates to 1500s.
6. Sushi, Japan: origins 4th century BC; Narezushi; Edo period (1603–1868).
7. Rogan Josh, Kashmir: Persian influence around 15th century; Kashmiri chillies.
8. Moussaka, Greece: roots in Ottoman Empire era.
9. Peking Duck, China: Yuan Dynasty; historian Wang Zhi, 2023 dissertation.
10. Borscht, Ukraine: 14th-century traditions; beetroot, cabbage, potatoes.
