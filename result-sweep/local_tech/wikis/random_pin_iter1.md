## 1. Smart Farming and Agriculture

### 1.1 Bob Anderson's Precision Farming Experiment (source: lmcousfp)
- Bob Anderson is a third-generation farmer from Springfield, Illinois. He initially saw his smartphone as a communication tool, not a farming tool.
- He attended the AgriTech Expo on October 3, 2023, where he saw drones for crop health, real-time soil sensors, and data analytics platforms.
- Funded by a grant from the Local Innovations in Farming initiative, he began his smart farming experiment in spring 2024.
- First step: adopting a Field Management Information System (FMIS).
- Soil sensors showed not all land needed the same water. Quote: "I was bringing the same rain to every part of my farm when some spots were crying for sunshine."
- Precision irrigation cut water consumption by 20% in the first year and improved yield.
- Drones identified pests and plant diseases early. Pesticide use dropped 35%, reducing costs and chemical runoff into the local water system.
- Claire Thompson, a data scientist specializing in agricultural analytics, analyzed the season's data. Quote: "the data from his farm is like a story."
- Following her advice on new crop varieties suited to soil conditions, yields rose 15%.
- Bob hosted community workshops; neighbors began exchanging data findings. Harvest season 2024 reported higher production and lower environmental footprint.
