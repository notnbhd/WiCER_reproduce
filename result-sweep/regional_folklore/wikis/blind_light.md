## Part I: Giants, Titans and Colossal Beings

### Giants and Titans: The Colossal Beings of Legend (overview, doc abkdxmnq)
- Describes giants and titans as original inhabitants of the world who predate gods and humans and shape landscapes.
- **Greek titans:** Gaia (Earth) gave birth to Uranus (Sky), who sired the first generation of Titans.
- Professor Phoebe Lykaios, University of Athens, lecture on **5 September 2023**.
- Oceanus governed the ocean; Rhea presided over the cultivated earth; Cronus devoured his children for fear of being overthrown, a prophecy fulfilled by Zeus and the Olympians.
- **Norse Jotnar:** Researchers from the Stockholm Institute for Nordic Studies published a paper on **12 October 2023** arguing the Jotnar were complex characters, not just brutish adversaries.
- The Jotnar were born from the primeval chaos Ginnungagap and were forebears of many gods.
- Frost giant **Ymir**'s body created the Earth; giants embodied ice, wind, fire and sea.
- **Stonish Giants of the Iroquois** (as named in the source): stone giants who sat in circles discussing rivers, forests and ecosystems.
- **Basque Jentilak:** introduced metallurgy to humans, then vanished into the earth at the dawn of humankind's age.
- **Dr. Sandra Mwende**, anthropologist, Cambridge Folklore Symposium, **18 November 2023**, on giants as creators and culture heroes.
- **David and Goliath** (Bible), dated c. **1000 BCE**.
- **Finn McCool vs. Benandonner** (Irish/Scottish): stone-hurling myth said to create the Giant's Causeway.
- **Professor Aarav Singh**, Calcutta Conference on Mythology, **15 January 2024**, on Asian giants.
- Hindu **daityas and asuras** are giant-like beings; **King Mahabali** was a pious, generous, just ruler.
- Modern works: film **"Titans Reawakened"** (director **Althea Shin**, December 2023), about Titans reclaiming Earth from ecological collapse; novel **"Gigantomachy: The Eternal Struggle"** by Jordan Abrams (released **23 September 2023**); video game **"Colossus Chronicles"** (award-winning).
