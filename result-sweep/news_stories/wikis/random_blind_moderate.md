## 1. Biodiversity, Climate, and Environmental Governance

### 1.1 Biodiversity Policy and Governance [aatdlexi]
- Anthropocene: proposed geological epoch in which human activity is the dominant influence on climate and environment.
- Paris Agreement on Biodiversity: dated December 12, 2025. Modeled on the Paris Agreement on Climate Change. Goal: halt biodiversity loss and restore ecosystem health by 2050.
- Post-agreement policy shift: localized strategies, community-led conservation, recognition of indigenous rights, biodiversity integrated into agriculture, forestry, and fisheries.
- June 2027: technology (satellite monitoring, AI data analysis, genetic technologies) routinely used for species tracking and database management. Enables "realtime global biodiversity plans."
- UNEP created the Biodiversity Council on November 30, 2028. Members include environmental scientists, indigenous leader Aiyana Quispe, business magnate Hiro Tanaka, and youth representative Malakai Johnson.
- 2030 Beijing Accord on Indigenous Knowledge: formal incorporation of traditional ecological knowledge into international biodiversity policy.
- As of September 2033: over 120 countries require indigenous consultative bodies to review biodiversity plans.
- Misconceptions addressed: studies published after September 1, 2023, found healthy ecosystems enable sustainable development; policies also aim to maintain genetic diversity and ecosystem functions.
- Corporate Biodiversity Credits: introduced March 15, 2026, function like carbon credits. VerdeTech Industries cited as an adopter.
- Strategic Framework for Biodiversity and Climate Adaptation: ratified April 5, 2031. Advocates "climate-proofing" through migration corridors and protection of climate refugia.
- Global Biodiversity Outlook, updated February 2034: estimated slowdown in extinction rates; cited American bison recovery and marine biodiversity rise from stringent fishing rules.
- Urban Greening Initiative (UGI): launched May 22, 2029. Focuses on native flora, green roofs, urban wetlands. City named: New Tokyo.

### 1.3 Coral Reef Restoration: The Ocean's Guardians [bxbwvhte]
- Dr. Elena Martinez, marine biologist with a newly minted Ph.D., joined the Great Barrier Reef Legacy in September 2023.
- Studies by late 2025: over 50% of the world's coral reefs had suffered bleaching (causes: warming, acidification, pollution).
- Coral Ark Project launched summer 2026 by Martinez. Cultivated heat-resistant coral strains in nurseries.
- By 2028: thousands of coral fragments reseeded via grafting, a horticultural technique.
- Community outreach taught coastal populations about reefs and offered alternatives to overfishing.
- World Coral Conservancy (WCC) established spring 2031; funded by philanthropic gifts and public investment.
- 2035: novel symbiotic breeding program; coral polyps cultivated in labs with symbiotic algae.
- Mid-2037: restoration progress noted; threats include new pollution and diseases.
