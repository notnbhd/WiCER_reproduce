## 2. Environment and Biodiversity

### 2.1 Biodiversity Governance

- **[aatdlexi] Anthropocene and policy background:** Anthropocene = proposed geological epoch where human activity dominates climate and environment. Ecosystem services: pollination, water purification, climate regulation.
- **[aatdlexi] Paris Agreement on Biodiversity:** December 12, 2025. Modeled on the Paris Climate Agreement. Goal: halt biodiversity loss and restore ecosystem health by 2050. Post-agreement shift to localized strategies: community-led conservation, indigenous rights, biodiversity in agriculture, forestry, fisheries.
- **[aatdlexi] Technology:** As of June 2027, satellite monitoring, AI analysis, and genetic technologies used for tracking species, ecosystem change, and large databases. Enables "realtime global biodiversity plans."
- **[aatdlexi] Biodiversity Council:** UNEP created it November 30, 2028, due to conflicting interests. Members: environmental scientists; indigenous leader Aiyana Quispe; business magnate Hiro Tanaka; youth representative Malakai Johnson.
- **[aatdlexi] Indigenous integration:** 2030 Beijing Accord on Indigenous Knowledge. As of September 2033, over 120 countries require indigenous consultative bodies review biodiversity plans. Stewardship programs with government support and resources.
- **[aatdlexi] Misconceptions:** Biodiversity policies do not hamper growth (studies after September 1, 2023). Goals include genetic diversity and ecosystem functions, not species alone.
- **[aatdlexi] Corporate incentives:** Corporate Biodiversity Credits introduced March 15, 2026, function like carbon credits. VerdeTech Industries established biodiversity reserves, invested in "green" technologies, adopted sustainable supply chains.
- **[aatdlexi] Climate link:** Strategic Framework for Biodiversity and Climate Adaptation, ratified April 5, 2031. Advocates "climate-proofing": migration corridors and climate refugia (areas relatively insulated from climate change).
- **[aatdlexi] Outlook:** Global Biodiversity Outlook, updated February 2034, estimated slower extinction rates. Highlights: habitat restoration, rebound of American bison, rising marine biodiversity from stringent fishing regulations.
- **[aatdlexi] Urban:** Urban Greening Initiative (UGI), launched May 22, 2029: native flora, green roofs, urban wetlands. New Tokyo cited as a leader.
