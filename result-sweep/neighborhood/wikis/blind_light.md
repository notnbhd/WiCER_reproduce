### 1.1 Fernandez Family Reunion, Mexico City
- Source: "Mexico City: Welcoming Family Legacies" (ayihnijq)
- Reunion dates: September 15, 2023 (preparations and food scents across the city); September 22, 2023 (main festivities).
- Family heritage dates from 1883, when patriarch Hector Fernandez laid the "cornerstone" of the family's heritage.

### 1.3 Tanaka Family Oshogatsu (New Year), Tokyo
- Source: "Tanaka Family New Year's Celebration: Preserving heritage" (ckfznxsm). Dateline: Tokyo, 10 September 2023.
- Family lives in the historic Asakusa District with an ancestral house.
- Members: Keiko Tanaka (matriarch), husband Hiroshi, children Yuki (eldest daughter) and Haruto (youngest).
- **Osoji:** year-end thorough cleaning to purify the home for the gods of the new year.
- **Kadomatsu** (bamboo and pine decorations) and **shimenawa** (sacred straw ropes) symbolize the home's sanctity and prosperity.
- **Osechi ryori:** food in multi-tiered *jubako* boxes. Kazunoko (herring roe) for fertility, kuromame (black beans) for health, datemaki (sweet rolled omelette) for knowledge.
- **Toso:** medicinal spiced sake drunk on New Year's Eve to ward off evil spirits and illness. Hiroshi prepares it, having learned the recipe from his father.
- **Joya-no-Kane:** family visits Senso-ji temple at midnight. The bell is rung 108 times for the 108 worldly temptations. Passes the Kaminarimon ("Thunder Gate").
- **Hatsumode:** first shrine visit.
- **Karuta:** family plays with hyakuninisshu classical poetry cards.
- **Kotatsu** (heated table) used for year-end reflection and future goals.
