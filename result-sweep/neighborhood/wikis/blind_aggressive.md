### Tanaka Family New Year's Celebration: Preserving Heritage [ckfznxsm]

  - Osechi ryori: food in multi-tiered jubako boxes. Kazunoko (herring roe) for fertility, Kuromame (black soybeans) for health, Datemaki (sweet rolled omelet) for knowledge.
