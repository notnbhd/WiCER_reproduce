## 3. Light Pollution

### 3.1 Light Pollution: Disrupting Ecosystems and Human Life (herylgfl)
- Dr. Evelyn O'Neill, New York City astronomer, at the International DarkSky Association conference October 24, 2023: "In some areas, our children might grow up without ever seeing the Milky Way."
- Research study September 15, 2023 by the Environmental Impact Group, led by ecologist Dr. Marcus Rivera, revealed startling effects of artificial lighting on migratory patterns, feeding behaviors, and reproductive cycles of wildlife.
- Birds navigating by stars become disoriented and fatally strike illuminated buildings. Newly hatched sea turtles misled by brighter shoreside lights rather than moonlight on waves. Pollinators such as moths and bats affected; food webs and pollination patterns undergoing an unpredictable overhaul.
- Dr. Grace Liu, sleep specialist, University of California, study November 5, 2023: light at night disrupts circadian rhythms, suppresses melatonin, potentially linked to certain cancers, obesity, depression, sleep disorders.
- Leonard Thompson, energy consultant, budget report December 12, 2023: "excessive and inefficient lighting costs municipalities across the globe billions of dollars annually."
- Jane Foster, CEO of LuminaTech: adaptive lighting systems.
- Actor and activist Mark Ruffalo endorsed Dark Sky Initiatives on social media September 22, 2023.
- RetroLux Solutions, CEO Aarav Singh: LED retrofits.
- Several cities enacted "lights down" laws as of September 30, 2023.
