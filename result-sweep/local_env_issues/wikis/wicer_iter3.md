## 1. Wetlands

### 1.1 Greenhaven and Hazelton Marsh [azzttqeo]
- Greenhaven community gathered at Hazelton Marsh on Sept 5, 2023 to mark the start of a wetland conservation chapter.
- Over 50% of America's wetlands lost since the 18th century (National Wetlands Inventory data).
- Dr. Susan Watkins, local environmentalist, interviewed Nov 10, 2023: "Wetlands are not just water bodies; they are the lifeline for numerous species, including humans."
- Mayor Thomas Eastwood launched the Greenhaven Wetlands Initiative (GWI) on Sept 22, 2023. Partners: Wetland Conservation Society; grants from the National Environmental Trust.
- John Cartwright, chief environmental engineer, said on Oct 15, 2023 that drones revolutionized conservation; drones used for topographical surveys, seed dispersal, and wildlife population monitoring.
- Dr. Kevin Lin (ecologist) said on Sept 30, 2023 that restoration requires understanding each wetland's ecosystem; his research guided native plant selection and water management to simulate natural hydrological cycles.
- Rachel Nguyen, economic advisor to GWI: return on investment in ecosystem services and community well-being is "immeasurable."
- Future plan: establish Hazelton Marsh as a research hub for wetland conservationists worldwide.
