## 1. Senior and Geriatric Health

### 1.1 Chronic Conditions and Geriatric Care

- **Personalized care plans and chronic disease tech (pilot programs):** Geriatric healthcare is evident in the integration of personalized care plans, the nurturing of physical and mental health in tandem, and the leveraging of technology to make managing chronic diseases more user-friendly for seniors. As we forge ahead, pilot programs and studies are in the pipeline to further explore and understand the complexities of aging and chronic disease management. Health experts are set to gather at the **International Conference on Senior Wellness and Chronic Disease Management on April 12, 2024**, to discuss and share innovative strategies to better serve our aging population.

## 4. Tobacco Control

### 4.1 Innovative Outreach (chrdeaym)

- WHO: tobacco kills more than 8 million people yearly; about 1.2 million deaths from secondhand smoke exposure.
- CDC: cigarette smoking causes more than 480,000 US deaths per year, including more than 41,000 from secondhand smoke.
- Global economic loss more than $1 trillion annually.
- **October 15, 2023**: Dr. Elizabeth Torres, public health expert, campaign using social media analytics on Instagram and Twitter to target young adults; preliminary **5% decrease** in tobacco initiation.
- **November 3, 2023**: Michael Lin, former smoker, non-profit app with machine learning to predict relapse.
- **December 10, 2023**: Springfield grassroots program; Dr. Karen Simmons; town halls, mobile clinics; **12% increase** in successful quit rates vs. previous year.
- **January 5, 2024**: federal bill raises tobacco purchasing age to **21** nationwide.
- **January 15, 2024**: significant tax increase resulted in **3% decline** in tobacco sales in first quarter.
- **February 20, 2024**: nationwide educational campaign by Department of Health with Prof. Anthony Rivera; interactive lifetime-consequence simulation modules.
- Jonathon Pierce, head of National Tobacco Control Program, analyzes findings.
