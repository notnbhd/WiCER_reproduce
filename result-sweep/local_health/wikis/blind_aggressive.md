## 3. Tobacco Control

### 3.1 Addressing the Tobacco Epidemic with Innovative Outreach
- WHO: tobacco kills more than 8 million people yearly; about 1.2 million are non-smokers exposed to secondhand smoke.
- CDC (US): cigarette smoking causes more than 480,000 deaths per year, including more than 41,000 from secondhand smoke.
- Global economic loss exceeds $1 trillion annually.
- **Oct 15, 2023**: Dr. Elizabeth Torres launched social-media-analytics campaign targeting young adults; preliminary 5% decrease in tobacco initiation among targeted group.
- **Nov 3, 2023**: non-profit led by **Michael Lin** (former smoker) launched app with machine-learning relapse prediction.
- **Dec 10, 2023**: Springfield grassroots program led by Dr. Karen Simmons; town halls, mobile clinics; 12% increase in successful quit rates vs. prior year.
- **Jan 5, 2024**: federal bill raises tobacco purchasing age to 21 nationwide.
- **Jan 15, 2024**: significant tax increase; 3% decline in tobacco sales in Q1.
- **Feb 20, 2024**: nationwide education campaign by the Department of Health with Prof. Anthony Rivera; interactive lifetime-consequence curriculum.
- **Jonathon Pierce**, head of National Tobacco Control Program, reviews findings.

### 3.2 Combating the Tobacco Crisis: A Vision for the Future
- Global Burden of Disease Study released Sept 7, 2023; tobacco a top risk factor for premature mortality.
- CDC, Sept 19, 2023: troubling increase in teen vaping.
- **Dr. Linda Reynolds**, Director of the Center for Tobacco Control Initiatives (Nov 12, 2023): embrace technology.
- **Tech-driven personalization**: machine learning predicts smoking patterns; December 2023 pilot showed 17% rise in successful quit attempts.
- **Social media influencers**: report by analyst Jacob Miles, Jan 15, 2024, on quitting journeys.
- **"Try Not to Smoke" AR campaign**: launched Feb 2, 2024; filter simulates years of smoking; piloted in schools.
- **Community-led initiative**: Oklahoma City, April 30, 2024; activist **Ellen Davis** trained community leaders.
- **QuitPlay app**: released June 21, 2024; rewards per smoke-free day; wearable integration.
- **CDC initiative** (July 15, 2024): free quitting aids through providers and community centers; Dr. Rodney Smith estimates up to 25% higher quit rates in underserved populations.
- **Tobacco-Free Generation bill**: to be discussed Aug 3, 2024; would raise purchase age to 25.
- Organizations cited: American Lung Association, National Institute on Drug Abuse (NIDA).
