## 3. Tobacco Control

### 3.1 "Addressing the Tobacco Epidemic with Innovative Outreach"
- WHO: tobacco kills more than 8 million annually; ~1.2 million from secondhand smoke.
- US CDC: cigarette smoking causes 480,000+ deaths per year, including 41,000+ from secondhand smoke.
- Global economic loss: more than $1 trillion annually.
- Oct 15, 2023: Dr. Elizabeth Torres introduced social media analytics campaign targeting young adults; preliminary 5% decrease in tobacco initiation among targeted demographic.
- Nov 3, 2023: Michael Lin, former smoker and advocate, launched quit-support app using machine learning to predict relapse.
- Dec 10, 2023: Springfield grassroots program led by Dr. Karen Simmons; town halls, mobile clinics; 12% increase in successful quit rates vs prior year.
- Jan 5, 2024: federal bill raised tobacco purchasing age to 21 nationwide.
- Jan 15, 2024: significant tax increase; 3% sales decline in Q1.
- Feb 20, 2024: national educational campaign by Department of Health with Prof. Anthony Rivera; interactive lifetime-consequence modules.
- Jonathon Pierce, head of National Tobacco Control Program, analyzes findings.

### 3.2 "Combating the Tobacco Crisis: A Vision for the Future"
- Global Burden of Disease Study released Sept 7, 2023: tobacco a top risk factor.
- WHO: 8 million+ lives annually. US smoking-related illness is leading preventable death cause.
- CDC (Sept 19, 2023): troubling increase in teen vaping.
- Dr. Linda Reynolds, Director of Center for Tobacco Control Initiatives (Nov 12, 2023): embrace technology.
- Tech-driven personalization: machine learning predicts smoking patterns; personalized messages showed 17% increase in successful quit attempts (pilot, Dec 2023).
- Social media influencers on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube; report by Jacob Miles (Jan 15, 2024) on influencers sharing quit journeys.
- **"Try Not to Smoke" AR campaign**: launched Feb 2, 2024; AR filter simulates smoking effects over years; piloted in schools.
- Oklahoma City: community activist Ellen Davis launched program April 30, 2024 training local leaders.
- **QuitPlay** app: released June 21, 2024; rewards non-smoking days; wearable integration.
- CDC initiative announced July 15, 2024: free quitting aids (nicotine patches, quitlines) through providers and community centers; public health analyst Dr. Rodney Smith estimated up to 25% quit rate increase in underserved populations.
- **Tobacco-Free Generation bill**: to be discussed in Congress Aug 3, 2024; would raise tobacco purchase and possession age to 25.
- Organizations: American Lung Association; National Institute on Drug Abuse (NIDA).
