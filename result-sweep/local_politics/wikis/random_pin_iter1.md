### 1.2 Open data portals

- **Rivertown (Mayor Elijah Warner):** "Rivertown Records" launched January 9, 2024, adding a public discourse forum. Civic groups tracked expenditures. Town hall attendance rose 20%. Warner press release February 28, 2024: "Rivertown Records is more than a transparency tool; it's a bridge between City Hall and the people we serve." [afewuoyn]
